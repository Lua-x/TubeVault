Family and living room: "Wer schaut?" (who's watching) instead of signing in on the TV, the phone as a remote, watching together in sync – plus seek previews, even volume, speed per channel and subtitles from speech recognition on your own server.

### New

- **"Wer schaut?" (who's watching)**: the TV and the family tablet become family devices and show profiles instead of the login – one tap and you're in. Every account decides for itself whether it appears there (the admin decides for kids profiles); admins and accounts with two-factor login only with a PIN. After five wrong PINs there is a 15-minute pause, and switching signs the previous profile out
- **Phone as a remote**: the TV shows a QR code and a six-digit code; with it the phone controls the TV view – pause, seek, back – and sends any video there with "Auf Fernseher" (to TV). Survives reloading the phone
- **Watch together**: one link, and everyone sees the same video at the same point. Anyone may pause and seek, and whoever falls behind is gently pulled back. Only those allowed to see the video get into the room
- **Seek previews** above the timeline, created once per video in the background (about 1–2 MB per hour)
- **Even out volume**: TubeVault measures the loudness of every video (EBU R128), and the player evens out loud and quiet videos – with a limiter against clipping. Not on iPhone and iPad
- **Speed per channel**: the player remembers the speed for every channel, in the audio player too
- **Subtitles from speech recognition** (faster-whisper): neither the program nor a model is in the image. Only a click on "Einrichten" (set up) loads the program from PyPI (about 450 MB) and a model of your choice from Hugging Face (75–485 MB) – or you place the model in `/config/models` yourself. Recognition then runs offline, at low priority on half of the cores: per video, for all your own videos without subtitles, or automatically after an import. New subtitles even appear in a video that is playing

### Improved

- Links in video descriptions are underlined; axe-core finds nothing on the 18 previous and the 8 new views (profiles, PIN, remote, TV pairing, watch together), in light and dark
- With 10,000 videos the new queries (video details, analysis and speech status) answer in 5–15 ms; the background analysis no longer slows requests down, even when files are missing

### Security

- One account can keep at most 20 rooms open (remote, watch together) – nobody can take them all; rooms everyone left close after ten minutes
- Pairing codes are valid for 10 minutes, wrong guesses slow the account down; rooms and codes live in memory only
- Speech recognition doesn't see passwords or client secrets from the environment, pip only installs prebuilt wheels, and Hugging Face is contacted without a login and with telemetry turned off

### Upgrade notes

- Two database migrations (0011 media analysis, 0012 family devices) run by themselves on start. A backup under **Verwaltung → Sicherung** beforehand never hurts.
- After the update, TubeVault measures the existing library bit by bit in the background – can be turned off under **Einstellungen → Medienanalyse** (settings → media analysis).
- For "watch together" over the internet, the reverse proxy has to pass WebSockets through (as it already does for live progress).

```sh
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
```
