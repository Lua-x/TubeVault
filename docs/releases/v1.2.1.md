Pictures without waking the disks: thumbnails and channel art now come from a small cache in `/config`, like Jellyfin's – the disks with the videos only spin up when someone watches.

### Changed

- **Image cache**: thumbnails and channel pictures are served from small copies in `/config/cache/images` – 640 px for cards and lists, 1280 px for the start page and the player poster. Browsing the library, the TV view and DLNA lists no longer read from `/media`, so pictures show at once even while the disks sleep. After the update the copies are made once for the whole library in the background (the disks are busy for a while then); later right after each download, while the disks are awake anyway. Copies of deleted or replaced pictures are removed by themselves
- **Browsers keep the pictures**: they no longer ask again after a day – the address changes when a picture does
- The daily check for **better quality** of new videos no longer looks at the file unless YouTube really has something better

### Upgrade notes

- No database migration; going back to 1.2.0 is possible.
- For the full effect `/config` should be on an SSD or the system volume and `/media` on the big disks – see [letting the disks sleep](https://github.com/Lua-x/TubeVault/blob/main/docs/installation.md#letting-the-disks-sleep). The cache needs a few dozen KB per video.
- A podcast app fetching a feed still wakes the disks, because the feed looks at the files.

```sh
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
```
