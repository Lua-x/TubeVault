Subscriptions and automation: TubeVault now downloads new videos on its own.

### New

- **Subscribe to channels and playlists** – new videos are downloaded automatically
- **Check interval per subscription**, from 15 minutes to weekly, plus "check now"
- **Filters per subscription**: Shorts, livestreams, minimum and maximum length, only videos after a date. Filtering happens on the channel listing and once more, exactly, after the metadata is loaded
- **Quality and format per subscription** (maximum resolution, prefer H.264, MP4/MKV)
- **Automatic clean-up**: delete videos older than X days or keep only the newest N. Videos added by hand and videos another subscription keeps are never removed
- **When subscribing**, download only the newest 5, 25, 100 or all existing videos
- **History per subscription** with status and reason (downloaded, filtered, skipped, removed) and "re-evaluate" after changing filters
- **Download queue**: pause and resume single downloads (partial files are kept), pause the whole queue, retry everything that failed
- **Channel avatar and banner** in the interface and as `folder.jpg`/`banner.jpg` for Jellyfin

### Upgrade notes

- The database is migrated automatically on start. Existing videos count as "added by hand" and are never cleaned up by a subscription.
- New menu entry **Abos** (subscriptions) in the sidebar and the tab bar.

```sh
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
```
