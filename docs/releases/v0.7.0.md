Listen and discover: play videos as audio only, subscribe to channels as podcasts, read comments offline – plus watch later, history, similar videos and search filters.

### New

- **Listen** ("Anhören"): on the video page and for playlists, only the sound plays. A mini player stays at the bottom while you browse; the full player has a timeline, ±15 seconds, previous/next video, speed (0.75× to 2×) and a sleep timer. The lock screen and headphone buttons control it, and progress is saved. The server only sends the audio track – AAC is copied out in seconds
- **Save to device as audio only**: about 1 MB per minute, plays in the same player without the server too
- **As a podcast**: channels and playlists (watch later included) as a feed for your podcast app. The address carries a personal key that only opens the feeds and can be renewed or turned off under **Einstellungen → Podcasts**; user rights apply here as well
- **Comments** (optional, off by default): save the top comments with their replies and read them offline – set globally, per subscription or per video, and "load comments" for existing videos
- **Watch later**: one fixed playlist per user, a tap on the video page is enough. Watched videos disappear by themselves (can be turned off), and there is a row on the home page
- **History**: everything started and watched, by day, removable one by one or all at once
- **Similar videos** from your library on the video page
- **Search filters**: length, upload date, channel and sorting – also without a search term
- Timestamps in descriptions and comments jump to that point in the player

### Fixed

- Two simultaneous progress updates for a video never watched before (e.g. seeking right after starting) caused a server error
- The hint in the "add video" dialog still referred to version 0.2

### Upgrade notes

- The database is migrated on first start (comments, watch later, podcast keys). Best back up under **Verwaltung → Sicherung** first – going back to 0.6 isn't possible afterwards.
- Comments and podcast feeds stay off until you turn them on. For podcast feeds behind a reverse proxy, set `PUBLIC_URL`.

docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
