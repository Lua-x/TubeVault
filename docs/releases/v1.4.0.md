The disks only wake when you press play, and one button looks for a better version of every video.

### New

- **Bessere Qualität suchen** (look for better quality): under **Verwaltung → Wartung** (admin area → maintenance) TubeVault asks YouTube about every video below its target quality – the subscription's limit, else 4K – no matter how old it is, and downloads again where a better version exists. The new file replaces the old one in place; watch progress, playlists and folders stay. It asks once per video and stops with a note if YouTube starts to throttle – simply start it again later

### Changed

- **Opening a video doesn't wake the disks**: the video page shows the picture with a play button and reads nothing from `/media` yet. The file, subtitles and seek previews are loaded once you press play – or a chapter, or a timestamp in the description. Autoplay in a playlist counts as pressing play. "Aufs Gerät" (save to device) asks about the file only when its dialog opens
