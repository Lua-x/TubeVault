Videos that YouTube only shows to a signed-in account – age-restricted, members-only and private ones – can now be downloaded with the cookies of your YouTube account.

### New

- **YouTube sign-in**: under **Verwaltung → YouTube-Anmeldung** (admin area → YouTube sign-in) you upload a `cookies.txt` of your YouTube account. TubeVault only uses it when YouTube asks for a sign-in: age-restricted videos, members-only videos, and private videos or playlists shared with you. Everything else – and YouTube's "not a bot" check – still runs without an account. The page shows until when the cookies are valid and when they were last needed. How to export them so they last: [administration guide](https://github.com/Lua-x/TubeVault/blob/main/docs/administration.md#youtube-sign-in-cookies)
- **The cookies stay private**: of the uploaded file, only the youtube.com and google.com cookies are kept, in `/config/cookies` and readable only by TubeVault – not in backups, never in the logs, and never shown in the browser again. What YouTube renews along the way is saved, so they stay valid longer
- **You hear when they expire**: by their date, or because YouTube ended the session – the admin area turns red, and the new notification "YouTube-Cookies abgelaufen" (YouTube cookies expired, on by default) says so once

### Changed

- A download that fails because YouTube wants a sign-in now says where to upload the cookies – or, with cookies, that the account has no access (e.g. not a member of the channel)

### Upgrade notes

- No database migration; going back to 1.2.2 is possible. Stored cookies then simply stay unused in `/config/cookies`.

```sh
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
```
