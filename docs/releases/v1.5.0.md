Autoplay with shuffle, a recommendation at the top of the library, and subscriptions that filter by title or save just the sound.

### New

- **Autoplay everywhere, with shuffle**: when a video ends, the next one starts after 5 seconds ("up next", can be cancelled) – no longer only in playlists. In a playlist it is the next one of the list, otherwise the first similar video you haven't watched. **Zufällige Reihenfolge** (shuffle) picks at random instead: from the playlist, or from the whole library – unwatched videos first, none repeated soon. Both are switches right next to "Ähnliche Videos" (similar videos) on the video page and under **Einstellungen → Wiedergabe** (settings → playback), saved per account
- **Empfohlen** (recommended) in the library: the big card known from the start page now opens the library too – with a video you haven't started yet, preferably from the channels you watch most

- **Title filter for subscriptions**: under **Filter** two new fields – **Titel enthält** (title contains): one of the words must appear, e.g. `Folge, Teil`; **Titel enthält nicht** (title doesn't contain): none may appear, e.g. `Trailer, Live`. Words are separated by commas, upper and lower case don't matter. Like the other filters it applies to the channel listing and again once a video's details are loaded; skipped videos show the reason in the subscription's list, and **Neu bewerten** (re-evaluate) applies changed words to them
- **Nur Ton** (sound only): a switch under **Qualität** (quality) makes a subscription save just the sound track as M4A – for podcasts, music or audio books. When YouTube offers AAC it is copied as it is, otherwise converted. These files play directly in the browser with the thumbnail as the picture, and in podcast feeds; resolution and format settings are hidden for such a subscription

### Changed

- The playback setting "Weiter mit nächstem Playlist-Video" is now called **Autoplay** and works outside playlists, too. It is on unless you switched it off – then it stays off

### Upgrade notes

- One database migration (0017) runs by itself on start. As always, back up under **Verwaltung → Sicherung** (admin area → backup) first – going back to 1.4 isn't possible afterwards.
