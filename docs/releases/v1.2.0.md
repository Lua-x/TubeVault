Your own folders to sort videos into, a sleep timer in the player, and a tidier video page.

### New

- **Folders**: under **Ordner** (folders, in the sidebar and among the library tabs on phones) you sort videos the way you like – "Kochen" with "Backen" inside, "Für die Kinder", "Lieblinge". **In Ordner** (into folder) on a video's page ticks the folders it belongs in; a video can be in several. Folders can hold folders (up to ten levels), and can be renamed, moved and deleted – the videos always stay in the library. Every account has its own folders; a kids profile only finds the videos of its channels in them
- **Sleep timer for videos**: the moon at the top right of the player – after 5 to 60 minutes or at the end of the video, the sound fades out over a few seconds and the video pauses. It works in full screen and keeps running into the next video of a playlist; "Ende des Videos" (end of the video) stops the playlist from going on. While it runs, the moon shows the time left

### Changed

- **The video page shows what matters most**: "Als gesehen markieren" (mark as watched), "Später ansehen" (watch later), "Zur Playlist" and "In Ordner" stay in the row; listening, watching together, saving to the device, downloading the file, YouTube and the admin actions (create subtitles, reload, delete) moved into the **⋯** menu. It works with the keyboard too and opens upwards when there is no room below
- **Downloads** always has a "Video hinzufügen" (add video) button at the top – before, it was only there while the page was empty

### Upgrade notes

- One database migration (0016) runs by itself on start. As always, back up under **Verwaltung → Sicherung** first – going back to 1.1 isn't possible afterwards.

```sh
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
```
