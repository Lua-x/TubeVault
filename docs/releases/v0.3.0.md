Library and playback: TubeVault remembers what you watch and now feels like a proper media library.

### New

- **Home page** with a big hero and the rows "continue watching", "new from your subscriptions" and "channels". The previous overview lives on as **Bibliothek** (library)
- **Watch progress per user**: videos resume where you stopped ("start over" is one click away) and count as watched shortly before the end. Marking as watched or unwatched by hand works too
- **Filters** for unwatched, in progress and watched in the library and on channel pages; progress bars and watched ticks on the thumbnails
- **Channel pages** with banner, avatar and all of the channel's videos – channels you don't subscribe to can be subscribed right there
- **Full-text search** over title, description and channel, with its own page and recent searches. Matches word beginnings and ignores accents ("brucke" finds "Brücke")
- **Your own playlists**: create, rename, sort by drag and drop and play in one go. A panel next to the player shows what comes next; after the end the next video starts after 5 seconds
- **SponsorBlock**: skip sponsors, intros, subscribe reminders and more while watching – with "back" in case you want to see it after all – or cut them out of the file when downloading. Globally or per subscription, with selectable categories. Off by default
- **Player**: SponsorBlock segments and chapters as markers on the timeline; notices and "up next" in full screen too
- **Installable as an app (PWA)** on iPhone, Android and desktop – with its own icon and without the browser bar
- **Einstellungen → Wiedergabe** (settings → playback) per user: continue playlists automatically, skip SponsorBlock segments automatically or with a button

### Upgrade notes

- The database is migrated automatically on start (progress, playlists, search index). Existing videos are added to the search index right away.
- The navigation has new entries: **Start**, **Suche**, **Bibliothek**, **Kanäle**, **Playlists** (home, search, library, channels, playlists).
- Android and desktop browsers only offer "install app" over HTTPS. On the iPhone, *Share → Add to Home Screen* always works.
- SponsorBlock stays off until you turn it on under **Einstellungen → SponsorBlock**. Only a short hash prefix of the video ID is sent to SponsorBlock.

```sh
docker compose pull && docker compose up -d
```
